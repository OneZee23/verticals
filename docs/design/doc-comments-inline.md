# Comments in a document's text

## Behaviour

- A comment sits on the words it is about: the words are marked, and the comment opens right over them, like a popover. The comments column on the window's right edge goes.
- While a document is open, above the field: a pill with the number of open comments, and Check again beside it.
- The count opens all the comments as a list over the field; a click on one takes you to its words.
- Check again hands the comments and edits to the agent, as the Go pill does today, and takes its place. The agent answers in the field in two lines, and what it rewrote is lit green in the document.

## Done when

UI tests: a comment opens over its words, the count and the list, a click in the list scrolls to the words, Check again sends the comments (with the fake agent) and the rewritten words light up. Before and after pictures.
